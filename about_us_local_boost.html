Our Mission

The heartbeat of
every city is its local business.

At Local Boost, we don't just build websites or run ads. We preserve legacies and fuel the future of the storefronts that make our neighborhoods feel like home.

Founded on Faith in Community

Local Boost was born in a small coffee shop three years ago. We watched as a beloved neighborhood bookstore struggled to navigate the digital shift. They had the soul, but lacked the digital megaphone.

We stepped in to help, and in six months, their foot traffic doubled. That’s when we realized: local businesses don't need "global solutions"—they need partners who understand their specific sidewalk, their specific street, and their specific regulars.

Today, we’ve partnered with over 150 local entrepreneurs to reclaim their digital space.
